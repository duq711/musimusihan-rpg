Run revision — 2026-10-06 / 질주 수정
The preserved fast-run capture now drives large forward/rear and vertical paw
trajectories rather than a common short swing arc. The cycle is retimed to 0.5s.
Further authored changes: extended/collected silhouette fitting, scapular glide,
forward forelimb reach, carpal/toe pitch, dynamic body clearance and contact polish.
Walk, care clips and the acquired model are preserved. The user-provided pose
reference remains local and is not distributed in this app.

Video-reference Run revision — 2026-10-06 / 영상 기준 질주 재수정
The final Run uses dog_fast_run_02_006.bvh frames 856..900 for source-derived
paw paths, retimed to a 0.70s displayed cycle. Authored changes include paired
contact timing, deep recovery, forward head/neck lean, continuous periodic body
curves, local shoulder fitting and smooth carpal/toe pitch. The video is a visual
guide; this is not a direct motion capture reconstruction of the YouTube dog.
Walk, care keys and acquired mesh/rest/weights/morph/coat mask are unchanged.
The YouTube reference is not downloaded or distributed in this app.
